# Scenario design notes

## Learner role

The learner plays Amina, a fictional Better Life Farming agri-entrepreneur in Tanzania's Lake Zone. She owns a rural BLF Centre, manages a small model farm, serves a network of smallholder farmers, and is completing ALP training in retail and business management.

This combines two documented roles:

- The BLF agri-entrepreneur is a trusted local business owner, input retailer, farmer adviser, and model-farm demonstrator.
- The ALP participant is expected to apply learning to their enterprise, supported by coaching and a business development plan.

## World assumptions

- Place: a fictional village in or near Bunda District in northern Tanzania.
- Season: early October, ahead of the vuli rains.
- Shop hours: the Centre opens around 7 AM and serves farmers through the day.
- Product mix: vegetable seed, fertilizer, registered crop-protection products, and drip-irrigation parts.
- Constraints: limited capital, costly delivery from Arusha, uncertain rain, seasonal demand, mixed customer liquidity, and the reputational responsibility of giving agronomic advice.
- Support network: a BLF agri-consultant, input distributor, model farm, demo days, crop calendar, ALP learning material, and the Better Life Farming chatbot.

## Decision-to-learning map

| Game decision | Evidence in the world | ALP learning surfaced |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Purchase and reserve seasonal seed stock | Stock card, recent sales, forecast, supplier terms, transport cost | Inventory Management Methods; Inventory and Business Cycles; Controlling Costs |
| Offer a farmer credit | Credit ledger, production plan, buyer contract, repayment capacity | Credit for Customers; Introduction to Credit; Customer Care |
| Respond to a crop problem | Leaf symptoms, product label, registration evidence, agri-consultant | Customer Care; Managing Risk; Business Relationships |

## Learning loop

1. Explore the Centre and gather evidence.
2. Commit to a business decision.
3. Observe financial, operational, and relationship consequences.
4. Receive a short field-guide explanation tied to an ALP topic.
5. Carry the changed business state into the next decision.
6. Review the season and replay the weakest decision.

## Content boundary

The game is a learning prototype, not agronomic, financial, or regulatory advice. Product registration, diagnosis, credit terms, and crop-treatment decisions should be localized and reviewed by Tanzania-based subject-matter experts before learner deployment.
